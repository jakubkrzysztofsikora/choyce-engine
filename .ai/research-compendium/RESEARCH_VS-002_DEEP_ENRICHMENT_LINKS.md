## BACKROOMS MONSTERS INTEGRATION SUMMARY

3. **Avoidable** - BACKROOMS triggers emit encounter_avoided signal, respect is_avoidable
4. **Clear Telegraphs** - BACKROOMS triggers create telegraph effects with configurable duration
5. **Parent Combat Gate** - BACKROOMS triggers check combat_gated and min_parent_approval
6. **Soft Aim Assist** - BACKROOMS triggers store aim_assist metadata for spawned creatures
7. **Reduced Damage** - BACKROOMS triggers store child_damage_multiplier for creatures
8. **Mood-Inspired** - BACKROOMS triggers use "Liminal Creature" naming (NOT "Backrooms")
9. **Grounded Collision** - BACKROOMS triggers use creature-specific collision sizes
10. **Physical Attacks** - BACKROOMS triggers spawn creatures with physical attack patterns
11. **Spatial Distribution** - BACKROOMS triggers enforce 200m+ from spawn
12. **Density Control** - BACKROOMS triggers enforce 1 per 500m radius
13. **Difficulty Levels** - BACKROOMS triggers support difficulty 1-3
14. **Child-Safe Audio** - BACKROOMS triggers use non-threatening audio cues
