# Review: Audio / Sound Design

## Summary
The audio stack is wired but broken on three independent axes: (1) ElevenLabs SFX peaks are
uncalibrated — `spawn_pop.mp3` hits 0.0 dB (digital clipping) while `block_buzz.mp3` peaks at
−41 dB (effectively inaudible); (2) every music loop has a 1.4–2.1 second silent tail that
destroys seamless looping under the "loop = true" path; (3) eight assets that exist on disk
are never triggered by any code, including both safety-block feedback clips (`block_oops`
voice and `block_buzz` SFX), so a kid who hits a moderation gate gets no audio cue. On top
of this, ~3.5 MB of Kenney CC0 OGGs and a Windows `desktop.ini` ship in `data/audio/sfx/`
but no code path ever loads them — pure dead weight.

## Findings (severity-ranked)

### Critical (block release)

- **C1: Safety-block has no audio feedback.** `block_oops.mp3` (voice "ojej, to nie jest
  bezpieczne…") and `block_buzz.mp3` (rejection SFX) exist in `data/audio/{voice,sfx/eleven}/`
  but are referenced from **zero** call sites in `src/` (`grep -rn '"block_oops"\|"block_buzz"'`
  → no matches). When the dual-filter moderation service blocks a kid's voice/text input, the
  kid sees a text message only. For a non-reading 4–7 year-old (target audience), that's a
  silent failure. Child-safety constraint in CLAUDE.md ("Prioritize child safety") makes this
  a release blocker.

- **C2: `block_buzz.mp3` is inaudible even if wired.** Peak −41.0 dB, mean −57.5 dB. The
  ElevenLabs adapter generated something that is essentially white-noise-floor. Even if C1
  is fixed by wiring the call, kids will not hear it through the −12 dB music bed and
  −6 dB SFX bus volume. Needs regeneration with a target peak of −3 dBFS like the other
  ElevenLabs SFX.

- **C3: `spawn_pop.mp3` clips (max 0.0 dBFS).** Sample peaks exactly at digital ceiling →
  audible distortion on every player spawn (gameplay enter). Re-render with −3 dBFS ceiling.
  Same issue with `victory_fanfare.mp3` (−0.9 dB) on celebration screen — plays simultaneously
  with `celebrate_win` voice, so summed peak will clip the master bus.

### High

- **H1: Music loops have silent tails (1.4–2.1 s).** All five `data/audio/music/*.mp3`
  tracks end with silence:
  - `mushroom_forest.mp3`: 1.94 s silence at end
  - `little_farm.mp3`: 1.73 s silence at end
  - `celebration.mp3`: 1.43 s silence at end
  - `landing_ambient.mp3`: 0.92 s silence at end
  - `adventure_island.mp3`: 2.07 s silence at end + 0.16 s silence at START
  AudioBank sets `stream.loop = true` (audio_bank.gd:95), so every ~30 s the ambient music
  drops out for 1–2 s before snapping back. This is the single most jarring audio defect a
  kid notices. Either trim the tails server-side or set `loop_offset` so the loop point
  skips the silence.

- **H2: SFX clips are 10–20× too long for UI events.** Every ElevenLabs SFX is exactly
  1.044898 s (or 2.037551 s for the longer ones). A UI click in a kid app should be 50–150 ms
  with a fast attack and quick decay. `ui_click.mp3` at 1.0 s has a mean of −54.6 dB → it's
  a barely-audible tap followed by ~750 ms of silence still occupying an SFX-pool slot. With
  `SFX_POOL_SIZE = 6`, rapid button mashing on the landing screen quickly exhausts the pool;
  AudioBank falls back to overwriting slot 0 (audio_bank.gd:69–71), cutting itself off mid-tap.
  Trim each SFX to its actual envelope or replace from Kenney's `kenney_interface/Audio/click*.ogg`
  (which are already on disk).

- **H3: Hover SFX flood risk.** `ui_hover.mp3` is also 1.044 s. `HOVER_THROTTLE_MSEC = 100`
  in AudioBank means a kid sliding a mouse across 6 buttons in 1 s can fire 6 overlapping
  1-second hover sounds across the SFX pool, drowning any concurrent click or footstep.
  Combined with peak −14.5 dB (loud) this turns a brief hover into a buzzing wall. Either
  bump throttle to ~250–300 ms or replace with a 40–60 ms Kenney `rollover` (also shipped
  but unused).

- **H4: No bus separation — all audio on Master.** `audio_bank.gd:39,45,52` sets all three
  player groups (music, voice, SFX pool) to `bus = "Master"`. Effects: kid cannot have music
  ducked under voice for clarity, parents cannot raise voice while keeping SFX quiet, and
  there is no compressor/limiter on a separate SFX bus to tame C3's clipping. The audio
  bank docstring promises "small pool of AudioStreamPlayers", but there's no project-level
  `default_bus_layout.tres` (none exists in repo), so even renaming `bus = "Music"` etc.
  would fail at runtime.

- **H5: World music does not stop when gameplay starts; victory_fanfare + celebrate_win
  stack on top.** `play_shell.gd:117` calls `_play_world_music(template_id)` before
  starting the session, but `_start_gameplay` (line 258) never calls `bank.stop_music()`.
  During the celebration overlay (lines 337–342), `play_sfx("victory_fanfare")` AND
  `play_voice("celebrate_win")` both fire while the 30 s loop continues underneath. Three
  −12-to-−6 dB streams summed → master clip. Compare with landing path which correctly
  calls `bank.stop_music(true)` when a world card is pressed (`landing_screen.gd:322`).

### Medium

- **M1: Dead voice clips (5 of 10).** `no_world.mp3`, `session_end.mp3`, `tools_unavailable.mp3`,
  `celebrate_collect.mp3`, and `celebrate_win.mp3` (only 1 use) exist with no/partial wiring.
  `session_end` voice is especially obvious — there is a session-end celebration screen but
  it plays `celebrate_win` not `session_end`. Either decide what each clip is for and wire
  it, or delete it. Files cost ~150 KB but the bigger cost is operator confusion: a future
  agent will assume "session_end exists therefore it is wired".

- **M2: Dead SFX (3 of 13).** `collect_star`, `footstep_sand`, `spawn_pop` never triggered.
  `sfx_player.gd:30` hardcodes `"collect"` → `"collect_coin"` only, ignoring star vs coin
  distinction. `footstep_sand` would require a surface-type check in `player_controller.gd`
  but `player_controller.gd:99` only emits a generic `footstep` signal with no surface
  metadata. `spawn_pop` is documented intent (player spawn) but `gameplay_runtime.gd:54–55`
  spawns a `sparkle_burst` particle effect with no audio cue.

- **M3: Dead music track (`celebration.mp3`).** Never referenced. The celebration screen
  uses `victory_fanfare` SFX + `celebrate_win` voice over world music — `celebration.mp3`
  would be the natural music swap but is unwired. 480 KB of unused data.

- **M4: 100 Kenney CC0 OGGs never loaded.** `data/audio/sfx/kenney_{ui,interface,scifi}/`
  contains 100+ pristine CC0 sounds plus license/desktop.ini junk. `grep -rn "kenney\|\.ogg"
  src/` returns zero matches. ~3.5+ MB of binary cruft in the repo. Either decide a subset
  is canonical (H2/H3 say yes, replace ElevenLabs UI clicks with Kenney clicks) or delete.

- **M5: `data/audio/sfx/kenney_scifi/Audio/desktop.ini`** ships in repo. Windows OS turd.
  `.gitignore` covers `.DS_Store` and `Thumbs.db` but not `desktop.ini`. Add it.

- **M6: Voice interruption with no fade.** `play_voice` (audio_bank.gd:82–87) hard-cuts any
  in-flight voice when a new one starts. Landing flow: greet_landing (3.3 s) plays at t=0.8 s;
  if the kid clicks ZAGRAJ at t=1.5 s, `world_picker` voice instantly cuts off mid-syllable.
  Better: fade-out 100 ms then start new voice, or queue.

- **M7: Music fade-out + immediate restart bug latent.** `stop_music` (audio_bank.gd:107–115)
  fade-out is 0.4 s; landing → world_picker click triggers `stop_music(true)` followed by
  PlayShell's `_play_world_music(...)` which calls `play_music(name, fade_in=true)`. If the
  PlayShell music starts before the landing stop fade completes, both tweens are running
  on the same `_music_player.volume_db` simultaneously. Godot will use the latest tween's
  values but the visible artefact is a volume jitter on shell transition.

- **M8: `parent_zone.mp3` and `world_picker.mp3` share identical file size (23449 bytes)
  and duration (1.4367 s).** MD5s differ, so they ARE different ElevenLabs renders, but
  the suspicious coincidence suggests the generator was given near-identical Polish prompts.
  Spot-check the actual speech — adversarial concern is that `parent_zone` and
  `world_picker` might say the same thing, leaving the parent-zone path without proper
  audible adult-only cueing. Cannot verify without playback ability in this worktree.

### Low / nits

- **L1: `AudioBank` and `ButtonFeel` autoloads have no `class_name`** by design (line 3 of
  each), but tests can't `class_name`-import them. Fine, but document the test-harness
  pattern (currently zero tests for AudioBank itself).

- **L2: AudioBank cached stream is shared and mutated.** `play_music` (audio_bank.gd:95)
  sets `stream.loop = true` on the cached resource. If anything else ever loads the same
  music stream resource (e.g. a future preview-mode tool), they get loop=true side-effect.
  Hold a `.duplicate()` per-call or set loop in `_load_music` once.

- **L3: `AudioEventBus.play_music` and `AudioEventBus.stop_music` signals are declared
  (lines 4–5) but never connected anywhere.** Dead signal API surface.

- **L4: `victory_fanfare` SFX duration 2.04 s, played via SFX pool with `bus = "Master"`,
  `volume_db = -6.0` — but it's effectively a musical sting, not a UI/gameplay SFX. Belongs
  on a `Music` bus or at minimum routed through `play_voice` for ducking.

- **L5: `victory_fanfare` is called twice in the codebase** — once via `sfx_player.gd:31`
  for "win" event and once via `play_shell.gd:340` for celebration. If gameplay victory
  triggers both paths (it does: `_trigger_victory` → `victory_sequence.play` → emit_sfx("win")
  → SFXPlayer → `play_sfx("victory_fanfare")`, then `_on_victory_completed` → `end_session` →
  `_on_session_ended` → `_show_session_end_screen` → `show_celebration` → `play_sfx(
  "victory_fanfare")`), the same 2-second sting fires twice within ~1 s. Will sound
  glitchy.

- **L6: Voice player volume_db = 0.0, music volume_db = -12.0, SFX volume_db = -6.0.**
  Hand-tuned, no source of truth. Build a `volume_config.tres` resource so designers can
  iterate without editing GDScript.

- **L7: No accessibility audio toggle.** Tasks/memory mention "narration toggle" landed
  in Wave A captions accessibility, but no toggle is wired to `AudioBank` to mute voice
  or music independently. If a kid has sensory sensitivity, parents cannot turn off the
  ambient loop without going to OS volume mixer.

## Recommendations

Prioritized punch list, by ROI for child users:

1. **Fix the loops (H1)** — trim 1–2 s of silence from each `data/audio/music/*.mp3`, or
   set `loop_offset` in `_load_music` after measuring. One ffmpeg pass + commit. Biggest
   sensory upgrade for the kid.
2. **Wire C1 (safety-block audio)** — in the moderation BLOCK path, call
   `AudioBank.play_sfx("block_buzz")` then `play_voice("block_oops")`. Then regenerate
   `block_buzz.mp3` to fix C2 (target −3 dBFS peak).
3. **Re-render or trim SFX (H2/C3)** — every ElevenLabs SFX needs a clean envelope.
   Either go back to ElevenLabs with `output_format=mp3_44100_192` and explicit duration
   targets, or use ffmpeg to trim silence and apply a limiter. `spawn_pop` must drop
   below −3 dBFS to stop clipping.
4. **Stop world music at session start, restore at session end (H5)** — one line in
   `play_shell.gd:_start_gameplay`: `var bank := _audio_bank(); if bank: bank.stop_music(true)`.
   Optionally restart `celebration.mp3` (M3) on victory.
5. **Bus layout (H4)** — create `data/audio/default_bus_layout.tres` with Music/Voice/SFX
   children of Master, set `ProjectSettings.audio/buses/default_bus_layout`, point each
   AudioBank player at its bus. Adds compressor on SFX to backstop C3.
6. **Throttle and shorten hover (H3)** — `HOVER_THROTTLE_MSEC = 250` and trim
   `ui_hover.mp3` to ~80 ms. Or swap to `kenney_ui/Audio/rollover3.ogg`.
7. **Decide on Kenney (M4)** — either delete `data/audio/sfx/kenney_*/` from VCS or pick
   ~12 sounds to canonicalize and delete the rest. Also `.gitignore` `desktop.ini`.
8. **Wire or delete the 8 unused clips (M1, M2, M3)** — `no_world` voice on
   `_show_no_world_cta`, `session_end` voice on celebration close, `tools_unavailable`
   voice on AI-tools-unavailable banner, `collect_star` SFX on a star-typed Area3D
   trigger, `celebration.mp3` music on `show_celebration`.
9. **Fade voice transitions (M6)** and **resolve music tween collision (M7)** —
   AudioBank could keep a `_music_tween` reference and `kill()` an in-flight tween before
   starting a new one.
10. **Accessibility (L7)** — add `AudioBank.set_voice_volume(db)`, `set_music_volume(db)`,
    `set_sfx_volume(db)` and wire to parent-zone preferences. Default voice on, music
    off if `parental_policy.sensory_quiet_mode == true`.
