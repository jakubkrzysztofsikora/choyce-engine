# Ground 003 PBR Material

Files in `data/textures/pbr/ground003/` are the 1K JPG maps for **Ground 003**
from [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Ground003).

- Source download: `Ground003_1K-JPG.zip`
- Maps retained: Color, NormalGL, Roughness
- License: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- Added: 2026-07-18

The material is used by the Adventure runtime ground plane.
