Glitch / CRT / scanline overlays
--------------------------------
Intended source: https://texture.ninja (CC0 photo-derived overlays)
License model: CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)
License URL: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The 7 PNG overlays in this directory are CC0 ORIGINAL PROCEDURAL WORKS generated
locally by scripts/assets/gen_glitch_overlays.py because texture.ninja's library
contains no glitch / scanline / CRT tags (verified against its /api/fetch_tags
endpoint at fetch time). All output is dedicated to the public domain.

Files:
- scanlines_fine.png, scanlines_thick.png — horizontal CRT scanlines
- crt_vignette.png                        — vignette + scanline layer
- glitch_rgb_shift.png                    — RGB-shift glitch bands
- static_noise.png                        — salt-and-pepper static
- neon_grid.png                           — magenta neon grid for floor decals
- vhs_tearing.png                         — VHS tear bands

Recommended use: alpha-blended on top of camera or world materials for phonk feel.
