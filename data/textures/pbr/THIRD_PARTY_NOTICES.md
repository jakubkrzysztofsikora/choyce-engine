# PBR texture sources

## Bark012

`bark012/Bark012_1K-JPG_Color.jpg`,
`bark012/Bark012_1K-JPG_NormalGL.jpg`, and
`bark012/Bark012_1K-JPG_Roughness.jpg` are from **Bark 012** by ambientCG.

- Source: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Bark012
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal
- Downloaded variant: 1K JPG

Only the albedo is currently used by the Adventure foliage material. The normal
and roughness maps are retained for the planned PBR foliage-material upgrade.
