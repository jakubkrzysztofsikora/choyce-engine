# 3D Asset Provenance

All assets in `data/models/kaykit/` and `data/models/quaternius/` are CC0
(no attribution required), sourced via Poly Pizza (https://poly.pizza/).
Direct itch.io downloads from KayKit / Quaternius were blocked by the
corporate network filter at the time of import; Poly Pizza mirrors the
same CC0 catalog (Kay Lousberg + Quaternius are listed contributors).

Styloo Animal Asset Pack (cat, dog, giraffe, chicken, cow, butterfly,
ladybug) is CC0 per https://styloo.itch.io/ ("Everything is CC0, Public
Domain, You can do whatever you want").
